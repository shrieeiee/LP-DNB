LP-DNB VERSION for Windows (64-bit)

  lp-dnb.exe     the groovebox
  lp-tenori.exe  the Tenori-on style sequencer

Unzip anywhere and double-click either one. Nothing to install.
Close one before opening the other: only one program can use the
Launchpad at a time.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because
the programs aren't signed: click "More info", then "Run anyway".

If a window ever stops responding, wait 10 seconds, then look in
  %APPDATA%\LP-DNB\
for lp-tenori-freeze-log.txt or lp-dnb-freeze-log.txt and attach it to
an issue at github.com/shrieeiee/LP-DNB/issues.
